## Delivered-locator and physical-reference evidence

The four private triage inventories enumerate existing evidence and review flags. A missing dependency keyword does not prove independence from the old benchmark. A missing separate rationale field or null normalized reference target does not by itself prove an index defect.

For every reused judgment, establish that the same delivered candidate path/reference and source page are under review; the exact frozen source eligibility and judgment rules apply; and its evidence and rationale answer the complete current requirement. Review flagged ambiguities, adverse or partial findings, policy-region questions and previous corrections explicitly. Retain an exact-ID compatibility disposition and artifact bindings. Reuse the existing evidence transparently where justified, distinguishing its original inspection from the current compatibility review. Otherwise conduct a fresh review. No bulk replacement of benchmark hashes, subject IDs or judgment labels establishes compatibility.

Physical reference destinations and typography remain candidate facts. Required reference obligations, their relevance and consequences must be reconsidered under the new benchmark and policy. Preserve uncertainty rather than manufacturing a destination or treating a normalization limitation as total wrongness.

For coordinated headings, establish the delivered meaning before assessing the atomic destination. V8.2 requires complete-path fidelity but does not define every lexical “and” as either a conjunction of required claims or an alternative-aspect category. A hypothetical `Railways > construction and maintenance` can fit a construction-only page if the delivered hierarchy and context independently establish that full label as a topical grouping, and the page accurately fulfills its scope. This is a conditional editorial interpretation, not an automatic umbrella exception. A heading promising a relationship, joint treatment or paired chronology still requires that promised meaning; other locators cannot repair an incomplete atomic destination.

Use the same approach for all four candidates and document the hierarchy/context evidence for the interpretation. The conjunction token, the usefulness of one aspect, and effects on scores do not decide it. Where the delivered context does not resolve the meaning, retain interpretive uncertainty for explicit adjudication. The existing `Paris > population and growth` example calibrates treatment depth and does not itself establish exact fit. Keep treatment, fit and consequence decisions separate: partial fit is not automatically no fit or a compound publication gate. This follows the existing [judgment policy](https://github.com/publication-intelligence/evaluate-subject-index/blob/7ed97581c1e82eb13052874b0e82eb429580a7b1/evaluate-subject-index/references/judgment-policy-v8.md) and records their underspecified editorial boundary without changing the frozen policy.

For passages continuing across a page break, immediate context may resolve an unambiguous referent when the atomic destination itself contains useful information in a predicate or consequence. Context cannot relocate useful treatment onto a destination containing only a subject fragment while all substantive information lies on another page. Assess the actual destination's treatment and complete-path fit separately; a page break alone establishes neither support nor absence. Preserve uncertainty where the reference remains ambiguous. This is an editorial inference from the existing useful-information and atomic-destination requirements, not a new page-context window, sentence-splitting algorithm or neighboring-page exception.

Preserve recognizable spelling or character corruption literally. When the delivered heading still identifies its topic unambiguously and its semantic scope is unchanged, exact destination fit can coexist with a separately justified heading or mechanics defect. This is an editorial inference from the distinct assessment components, not an automatic typo exemption or penalty. Do not infer the delivered meaning merely by guessing a correction. Materially obscured or changed meaning may justify nonexact fit or uncertainty; assess the actual retrieval consequences and keep structured error codes consistent with the assigned axes. A defective-but-identifiable cross-reference destination is a separate category and does not automatically make an atomic locator partially supported.
