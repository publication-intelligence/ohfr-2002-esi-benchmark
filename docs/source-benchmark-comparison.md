Verified source-meaning, relationship and task defects occur in both. The evidence supports doing the reconciliation work; it does not establish an overall quality ranking or approve every record in a proposed successor.

The comparison uses methodology commit `c11c6ccb16000fe79646af16b7be01f6cbeeac78`.

That [targeted check](../validation/reconciliation/source-review/independent-decision-check.json) is distinct from the complete independent review still required for a successor.

**Why unchanged adoption fails.** On page 151, legacy `SUBJ-20E4EBF24394` has been narrowed to preservation of slavery, duplicating `SUBJ-5EF2BDD9F1CB`, but still carries evidence about the different struggle for free-coloured political rights. Its incident edges were not consistently revised: `REL-40DAB00EC7B6` makes Robespierre support the narrowed preservation-of-slavery subject, while `REL-F901AD713C65` places free-coloured rights beneath it. `REL-A189108B8550` links the duplicate records and `REL-728B1FB47A0B` gives Barnave an incompatible contrast. The independent incident-edge check also identifies stale `REL-028476B899D6` and a direction mismatch in `REL-CAB4FAA85E16`, while retaining `REL-16B98BBC3184` as locally supported. This is a concrete final-record defect, not a preference for fewer subjects. Native `SUBJ-0209` preserves the two source treatments coherently, although nearby `SUBJ-0208` overstates the evidence by describing Robespierre's positions as anticolonial rather than the narrower antislavery and racial-political-rights positions established here.

On page 275, native `SUBJ-0448` and `EVID-0448-002` lose the author's qualified inference about a changed phase of the Terror. The passage argues that the June–July pattern moved unusually close to social discrimination while acknowledging that many victims may also have committed subversive acts. The native phrase about class alone can preserve part of that caveat; the problem is its displacement of the phase-specific inference and the evidence summary's added, unsupported counterargument about discrimination against lower classes. Legacy `SUBJ-04661509BFF9` preserves the distinction better. Yet legacy `REL-B0C022DE2900` reverses its discovery relation: the Great Terror remains the source and the Prairial law the target while `enabled_by` becomes `enables`.

Other source checks show a legacy western-pacification task, `TASK-9D06A030D5FC`, routed to unrelated subjects instead of the treaty and western-resistance material on pages 289–290. Native `REL-0244` gives a repeal relation an overbroad compound antecedent containing conscription as well as the forced loan and Law of Hostages; pages 371–372 and 384 identify repeal of the latter two while continuing to discuss conscription. Native `SUBJ-0598` silently resolves the internally inconsistent purge date on pages 389–390; legacy `UNC-E6DC0EA51B05` retains that uncertainty. These findings require precise repairs and dependency review. They do not invalidate every historical judgment or prove that every relationship has the same defect.

**What each lineage contributes.** Tested native consolidations often preserve a coherent source argument. Its Fructidor unit retains both the coup and the qualified possibility of constitutional accommodation; its bankruptcy unit preserves fiscal relief and breach of public faith; its currency unit distinguishes assignats and territorial mandates. Adding every corresponding legacy record would duplicate some reader needs. Conversely, legacy often supplies valuable direct routes or qualifications that broad native units leave implicit: the censorship-publicity paradox, permanent financial audit, factory discipline, women’s popular price action, free-coloured citizenship, different restoration-of-slavery outcomes, and the concluding human-cost reckoning. Those are source-grounded refinements, not proof that every finer legacy subject needs its own weighted requirement.

Priorities must be reconciled independently of counts. The same Chouannerie treatment is major in legacy and essential in native; the women/equality treatment changes in the opposite direction. Splitting the Declaration or consolidating constitutional debates can redistribute weight without changing treatment. Separately weighting illustrative episodes, such as the burning of assignat plates, can duplicate a monetary-process requirement. These remain editorial questions requiring whole-book justification. Meaning, principal versus supporting treatment, distinct reader purpose and dependency should decide them.

Reader tasks need the same discipline. Tested native tasks use a general argument-and-qualification template and are answerable, but often fail to test the distinctive contrasts exposed by the source. Some legacy questions do that well; others are equally generic or have bad links. A false fallback flag, a populated question field or one task per subject does not establish an independently useful scholarly task.

**Concrete successor plan.** Use native v2's organizing units as a provisional drafting scaffold where the tested groupings preserve meaning. That is a convenient source architecture, not study-wide adoption or a claim about unreviewed native records. Keep both frozen releases and all original discoveries/reviews unchanged. Create a separate draft lineage and exact field-level change ledger; record stable-ID additions, removals, updates and every proposed split/merge.

1. Define one coherent retrieval requirement for each materially distinct source argument. Keep component aliases and evidence when another weighted subject would repeat the same reader need. Split only where different reader purposes or source qualifications require it. Do not union the two inventories.
2. Correct the demonstrated defects in both lineages, including every incident edge and task link affected by scope changes. Preserve the Great Terror qualification, precise racial/legal categories and geographical outcomes, source-date uncertainty, and the demonstrated direct access routes.
3. Reconcile all 17 discovery packets against the full source. Inspect the 297 currently unreviewed pages; review every unreviewed subject and all remaining evidence, relationship, task and uncertainty fields. Revisit all cross-chapter groups, priorities, redundant requirements, label-only relationships and generic tasks. The complete entity queue is in the coverage ledger.
4. Conduct a fresh complete source-first omission pass for arguments, processes, institutions, groups, places, events, historiographical distinctions, causes, consequences and conclusions. Current probes establish specific defects; they do not establish the absence of other omissions.
5. Submit the actual completed draft and exact change ledger to a separate fresh candidate-unexposed context for an item-complete independent review. Only an approved, validated draft can be frozen and considered for study-wide adoption. This bounded comparison and the separate targeted decision check cannot substitute for that review.

The current findings do not require a user preference to begin this source-grounded drafting. Routine granularity and priority decisions can be proposed with evidence and resolved in independent review. No candidate migration, compatibility approval or transfer of prior audits is approved here.

**One reproducible density basis.** Both historical 17-chunk totals mix different extraction, token, chapter-title, note-callout and map rules. Legacy totals 194,718 and native 195,346; neither total chooses the benchmark. A uniform source-only protocol now yields **193,118 words**, with exact counts for every page and chunk in [the measurement](../validation/reconciliation/source-review/source-density-measurement.json). Its computation is explicitly unregistered; [the separate protocol review](../validation/reconciliation/source-review/source-density-protocol-review.json) records the source-review rationale.

The protocol counts body prose, block quotations, available substantive notes and substantive map captions. It excludes running furniture, chapter display navigation, note-callout numerals, source credits and map internals. The 48-name numbered list on page 129 functions as a map key; it remains semantic evidence but is excluded from this prose denominator. NFKC normalization and one whitespace/alphanumeric rule handle the text uniformly, with line-wrap hyphenation joined. The geometry is bound to the exact source, manifest and page map; source roles, not a preferred total, determined inclusion. This is an operational extraction count, not a claim to uniquely correct linguistic segmentation.

Reproduce it with the [public source-only script](../scripts/measure_source_density.py), keeping extracted XML in a restricted directory:

```bash
python3 scripts/measure_source_density.py \
  --source SOURCE.pdf \
  --manifest chunk-manifest.json \
  --page-map page-map.json \
  --private-dir PRIVATE_WORK_DIRECTORY \
  --output source-density-measurement.json
```

The script checks exact source bytes, exact manifest/page-map bytes and recomputed canonical identities before writing a measurement. The coordinator independently reproduced the final JSON byte-for-byte and verified that a changed manifest is rejected before output creation. The measurement and protocol review change no historical count, frozen benchmark or policy. Public artifacts contain analytical summaries and IDs; extracted source text and working packets remain restricted.
