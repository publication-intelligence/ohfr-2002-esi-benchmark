3. Record page regions by role and coordinates. Include substantive body text, block quotations, substantive inspectable footnotes and captions. Exclude folios, repeating heads, isolated note callouts with absent notes, scale numerals, and purely navigational cross-references. Retain chapter title words as chapter text consistently, excluding the bare chapter number. Hash the transcription and have it visually checked. Never claim either historical total proves that choice.

Use one exact token rule: after approved line-wrap repairs and whitespace collapse, a word is a maximal whitespace-delimited token containing at least one Unicode letter or decimal digit; count standalone punctuation as zero. Keep internal apostrophes and genuine hyphens as one token, numeric values as one. Maintain an explicit page/line repair ledger for joined soft or line-end hyphenation; preserve genuine lexical hyphens. Do not silently infer all end-line hyphens are soft.
6. Write per-page included/excluded counts, normalized-text hashes, repair/transcription manifest hashes and chapter totals; independently rerun to the same counts. Verify sums and ownership. Publish only totals and method metadata; retain extracted source and ledgers privately.
